Threads Analyzer

Pulls every post from a Threads account through the Meta Threads API and turns them into a 12-sheet Excel report. Because it calculates the median instead of the average, you can see performance by time of day that a single viral post would otherwise hide.

The command line CLI is free and open source and is available now. A desktop app that can be used with a click without installation is being prepared, and the launch price is $29 (regular price $39), a one-time payment. You can get it for $19 if you join the waitlist.

12 sheets in the report

Once you run it, an Excel file containing one account will appear.

  • All Posts: Date, views, likes, and engagement are all included in each post and can be filtered.
  • Insight Ranking: Top 30 are selected based on views, engagement, like rate, and virality.
  • Time of Day analysis: Crosses 24 hours and the day of the week to produce the median and dead rate.
  • Daily view count trend: Daily time series of up to 730 days and 7-day moving average.
  • Snapshot growth trend: Saves the follower count on every run. The API keeps no history, so the app records it itself.
  • Viral in-depth analysis: Calculate the top 1% based on account size.
  • Content Optimization: Performance by number of characters and topic tags.
  • The remaining five sheets are Like Rate, Monthly Trends, Follower Demographics, Growth Insights, and Growth Strategy.

What you need before using it

  • You must create the Meta developer app yourself. Meta opens insights only through apps created by the account owner. Buying the desktop app does not remove this step; it takes about 10 minutes with the on-screen wizard. If that sounds like too much, we recommend not buying it.
  • The follower demographics sheet only appears when you have 100 or more followers, due to Meta policy. The remaining 11 sheets just need posts.
  • Time-of-day and character-count analyses give a verdict only for ranges with 30 or more samples.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does my data go?

It isn't sent anywhere. There is no server: the Meta API is called directly from your computer, and the resulting Excel file is saved only on your computer. The token is kept in a local .env file, with its permissions locked to 600.

How long does it take?

The first collection takes about 10 minutes for 2,000 posts. From the second time, it uses the cache and takes 1 to 2 minutes.

Can I use it if I have only a few followers?

Yes. If you have less than 100 followers, only the Follower Demographics sheet will be blank, and the remaining 11 sheets will remain as is.

Why is the app paid when the CLI is free?

The engine is the same; the app just saves you from setting up a terminal, Python, and certificates. If you don't mind spending that time, the free version is all you need.
