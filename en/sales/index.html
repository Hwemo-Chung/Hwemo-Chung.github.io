Policy timeline and real-world impact

Diagnosis and estimates are based on the contents of the official documents below.

  • Apple SDK, from 2026-04-28. Upload requires Xcode 26 and iOS 26 SDK and may prevent hotfix submission. Apps that are already installed will not be deleted from your device.
  • Play target API, 2026-08-31 (extension application until 11/1). New apps and updates require API 36. If your existing app is below API 35, its availability to new users on OS devices higher than your app target will be limited. It is not blocked for all new users and devices worldwide.
  • Firebase Dynamic Links, shut down 2025-08-25. page.link Their links may return 404. They are only worth recovering if they still bring in traffic, and not all apps are losing sales right now.
  • Cordova, no date set. Plugins and an outdated CLI can make it difficult to keep up with the latest SDK. There is no reason to believe that it has been officially archived or has gone into maintenance mode.

Source: Apple, Google Play, Firebase Dynamic Links FAQ
