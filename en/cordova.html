Diagnose Cordova/Ionic apps that are blocked from updating iOS

Starting April 28, 2026, your app will need to be built with Xcode 26 and the iOS 26 SDK to be eligible for App Store Connect. Older Cordova/Ionic build environments fall prey to this requirement first. We will only look at the publicly available store information and explain what is blocked. It's free for the first 10 companies, and we'll respond within 2 business days.

Free diagnostics just clears up what's blocked. Fixing methods and operations are paid. No source code, signing key, or store account required.

Three things to diagnose

Apple SDK Minimum Requirements Applicable from 2026-04-28

App Store Connect uploads require Xcode 26 or higher and the iOS 26 SDK. old cordova-ios or older build pipelines may prevent update submission. Apps that are already installed will not be immediately removed from your device.

Google Play Target API 2026-08-31, extension application until 11/1

New apps and updates must target Android 16 (API 36) or higher. There are exceptions for each form factor. If your existing app targets Android 15 (API 35) or lower, its availability will be limited to new users on devices with an OS higher than your app's target. Not all users and all devices will be blocked. Depending on the app, you may be able to request an extension until 2026-11-01 in the Play Console.

Firebase Dynamic Links Service ends on 2025-08-25

According to the Firebase official FAQ, the service has been terminated. page.link If the same address returns 404, the link is most likely dead. If a link has been left unattended for nearly a year, it may not have received any inflow to begin with. So, in the diagnosis, we look at whether there are dead links and the size of the inflow separately.

Cordova·Ionic apps are often tied to native plugins and outdated CLIs, making it difficult to keep up with the latest Xcode and Play targets alone. However, this does not mean that Cordova is officially archived. Each app has different circumstances.

Apps that fit and apps that don't fit

  • B2C app or external app that requires new installation, membership registration, or recruitment
  • App that requires iOS hotfix but has broken build
  • Cordova/Ionic app discontinued by original developer
  • Teams with no or only one in-house mobile manager

Apps used only by field staff with no new installs may be little affected by Play policy. If so, the diagnosis says so plainly.

How to check directly

You can check iOS recent uploads first, then Play target, and deep link if applicable. Deep links can be confirmed in 30 seconds without a developer, but don't assume a loss of sales just by looking at 404.

  • iOS upload. Check the App Store or in-house build history to see if there have been any successful uploads or deployments since 2026-04-28. If there are no records of success, your upload may be blocked. These are the items that have the greatest impact.
  • Cordova or not. Unzip the APK assets/www/cordova.js or cordova_plugins.js exists. If it does, it's most likely Cordova-based.
  • Play target API. In the result aapt dump badging app.apk Look at targetSdkVersion. If it is less than 35, the offer may be limited to new users of higher OS devices after 8/31. Apps that are only used by employees who have already installed them may have less impact.
  • Deep linking. Among app links on homepage, QR, text, and email Paste the page.link address into your browser. If it's 404, the link is dead. You need to check separately whether there has been an inflow.
  • Period of neglect. View the last update date for the store. The older it gets, the more work there may be in your build environment. Don't judge by date alone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the diagnosis free?

It costs very little because it only looks at public sources. The first 10 companies are free, to build references, after that there is a fee. If not applicable, reply as not applicable. Detailed editing instructions are provided only at the paid level.

Do I need to give the source code or signing key?

Not required for diagnosis. Repository access may be required during the implementation stage, but the store signing key and Play/App Store account can be designed to remain in the customer's possession. Basically, we recommend delivering the code and building it in the customer's build pipeline.

Are apps used only by field employees also affected by Play policies?

If there are few new installs and only existing devices are installed, the impact of limiting new user provisioning may be small. The bigger problem with these apps is that they cannot update iOS hotfixes and security patches. When diagnosing, we will ask whether you are distributing through an in-house MDM or installing a new product through the store, and we will tell you accordingly.

Can’t I just apply for an extension?

Extending Play only buys time. According to official information, you may be able to apply until 2026-11-01. The requirements do not go away and go up every year. There is no extension period for iOS SDK requirements.

If I move to Capacitor, do I have to rewrite my web code?

Most of the time, it is written as is. What we change is the native runtime and plugins. Having outdated Angular or obsolete plugins increases man-hours. A list of problematic items will be included in the diagnosis, and instructions on how to fix them will be provided during the paid design stage.

Can I hand the signing key to an outside studio?

Not recommended. We recommend a contract that divides the scope into stages and leaves signing and submission to the store under the client's authority.

Can’t the deep link be replaced with a new link?

You can change the link to be exported in the future. Printed materials, texts, and URLs of external sites that have already been distributed cannot be retrieved, so server redirection is required. If there is no influx, there may be no reason to spend money on restoration.

Can this be solved if I use Branch or AppsFlyer?

Both services are tools for creating new links. It does not automatically revive old addresses that are already dead. Setting up a redirect and replacing the link creation part of the app code are separate tasks.

Can Japanese companies also request it?

Practical response is available in Japanese. You can also send inquiry emails in Japanese.
