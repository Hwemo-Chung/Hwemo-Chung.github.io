How it works

  • 1. Fit email. You send the product, the platform and one issue class. We confirm the fit or decline in writing.
  • 2. Pilot week. $400, about 6 hours, diagnosis plus a fix attempt. You pay the invoice before work starts.
  • 3. Retainer, if you want it. If the pilot helped, move to Stabilizer or Scale with a monthly hour cap. Cancel at the end of any month.

Who this is for

Teams with a live or about-to-ship Ionic, Capacitor or WebView app, where the problems show up on devices, in the stores or at release, not in mockups. You can pay in USD and are fine working async in English.

We do not take full rewrites, unlimited Slack, greenfield apps at hobby rates, or work that conflicts with existing employment or confidentiality obligations.

FAQ

What do I get from the $400 pilot?

A written diagnosis of one agreed issue class and a best-effort fix within about 6 hours. If the fix needs more time, we send an estimate before going over.

Do unused hours roll over?

No, unless we agree in writing beforehand. The cap keeps both the schedule and the price predictable.

How do I pay?

By invoice, before work starts: card (Stripe or Lemon Squeezy once linked), Wise or bank transfer. Reply to the fit email and we will send payment instructions for your region.

Do you give refunds?

Work starts after payment, and hours already used in the week are not refunded. If we decline before you pay, you pay nothing.

Can you work on my employer's confidential stack?

Not if it conflicts with existing employment obligations or an NDA. Tell us up front and we will decline.

Why a specialist studio on retainer instead of an agency?

You buy a narrow outcome: one issue class, or a month with an hour cap. The scope is written down before any hours start, and you can cancel at the end of the month.
