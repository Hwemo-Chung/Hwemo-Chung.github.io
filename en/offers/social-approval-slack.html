Post approval bot (Slack/Mail)

When the draft arrives, click Approve or Reject in Slack or email, and the results are left on the sheet.

This product only creates an approval flow, and actual posting is done by the person in charge or linked to the official scheduler. It is suitable for organizations where stationery inspection is difficult, such as hospitals, clinics, and finance.
