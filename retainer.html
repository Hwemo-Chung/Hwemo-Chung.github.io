FAQ

What do I get from the $400 pilot?

A written diagnosis of one agreed issue class, plus a best-effort fix within ~6 hours. If the fix needs more time, you get a clear estimate — no silent overrun.

Do unused hours roll over?

No, unless we pre-agree in writing. The cap protects both schedule and price.

How do I pay?

Invoice before work: card processor (Stripe / Lemon Squeezy when linked), Wise, or bank transfer. Reply to the fit email and I send payment instructions for your region.

Refunds?

Work starts after payment. No mid-week refund once hours are used. If I decline the fit before payment, you pay nothing.

Can you use my employer’s confidential stack?

No if it conflicts with my employment or NDA. Say so up front — I will decline cleanly.

Why trust a single operator?

You buy a narrow outcome (one issue class / hour-capped month), not a brand agency. Scope is written before hours start. You can cancel at month end with no lock-in.
