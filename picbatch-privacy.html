PicBatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

PicBatch is a batch photo processing app for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and — more importantly — what it never does with that data.

The short version

Everything happens on your device. PicBatch has no servers, no accounts, and transmits nothing.

Photos

  • PicBatch only accesses photos you explicitly select through the Android Photo Picker. The app holds no storage permission and cannot browse your library on its own.
  • Selected photos are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone, including us.
  • Processed results are saved as new files under Pictures/PicBatch. Your original photos are never modified or deleted.

Face detection

  • The optional face masking feature detects faces on your device using Google's ML Kit with a bundled model. Images are not sent to Google or anywhere else for detection; the ML Kit face detector used by PicBatch runs fully offline.
  • Detected face data (bounding boxes) is used only in memory, only to apply the mosaic you requested, and is discarded immediately afterwards. No biometric data is stored, and no facial recognition (identifying who a face belongs to) is performed — only detection of where faces are.

Photo metadata (EXIF)

  • If you enable "Keep photo metadata", capture metadata (date, camera settings) is copied from the original to the processed file — on device, as part of writing the file you asked for.
  • Location (GPS) metadata is removed by default. It is only preserved if you explicitly turn on "Include location".

What we collect

Nothing. PicBatch does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, usage analytics, crash reports, identifiers, or telemetry of any kind.

If a future version adds advertising or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the in-app disclosure will change accordingly.
